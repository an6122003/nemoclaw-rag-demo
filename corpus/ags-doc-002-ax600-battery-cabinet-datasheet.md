## 1. Description

The AX-600 is the larger of the two AX-series LFP battery cabinets. It is a liquid-cooled, single-enclosure unit intended for utility-scale and large commercial deployments where higher energy density per pad footprint is required. It is commissioned with a Comet C2 site controller and Helios H3 power conversion systems.

Operators frequently confuse the AX-600 with the AX-400 because the two cabinets share enclosure finish, communications protocols and cabinet width. They do not share energy, DC bus voltage, module part number, cooling method, ingress rating or DC lug torque. Confirm the nameplate part number (AGS-AX600-558) before ordering spares or applying torque values.

## 4. Communications and compliance

The AX-600 exposes Modbus TCP and CAN 2.0B. Coolant flow, coolant pressure and pump status are published to the Comet C2 site controller in addition to cell-level telemetry. Certified to UL 9540, UL 1973 and IEC 62619.
