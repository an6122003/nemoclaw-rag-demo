# Transport, Storage and Receiving Guide

**Document ID:** AGS-DOC-028
**Revision:** C
**Effective date:** 2025-06-05
**Applies to:** AX-400 (AGS-AX400-372), AX-600 (AGS-AX600-558), Helios H3, Comet C2

## 1. Shipping configuration

AX-series cabinets ship as fully assembled units on a dedicated steel skid, charged to **40% state of charge** and with the DC disconnect open and tagged. The shock indicator on the door reads untripped when the unit has not been subjected to an impact above 5 g.

| Product | Shipping weight | Transport class |
|---|---|---|
| AX-400 | 2,850 kg | Class 9, UN3480 (UN3481 when in equipment) |
| AX-600 | 4,180 kg | Class 9, UN3480 (UN3481 when in equipment) |
| Helios H3 | 620 kg | Not regulated |
| Comet C2 | 3.2 kg | Not regulated |

Battery modules shipped loose are **Class 9, UN3480** and must be at or below 30% SoC.

## 2. Transport requirements

1. Use a carrier approved for Class 9 dangerous goods and provide the UN 38.3 test summary.
2. Secure the cabinet with four rated chains or straps to the transport deck. Do not secure over the door or the door hinges.
3. Do not stack AX-series cabinets. The Helios H3 may be crated two high.
4. Limit road transport shock to 5 g and vibration to 1 g RMS in the 5-200 Hz band.
5. Do not transport a cabinet that has been energised without first confirming the DC disconnect is open and the cabinet is at rest.

## 3. Receiving inspection

1. Photograph the skid, the shock indicator and all four sides of the cabinet before unpacking.
2. Confirm the nameplate part number matches the packing list. An AX-600 is 558 kWh and reads **AGS-AX600-558**; an AX-400 is 372 kWh and reads **AGS-AX400-372**.
3. If the shock indicator is tripped, do not energise the cabinet. Raise an RMA and await an AGS inspection.
4. Check the AX-600 coolant sight glass for transit leakage and confirm the coolant level before accepting the unit.
5. Record any damage on the carrier's delivery document before signing.

## 4. Storage before installation

Store indoors or under a ventilated cover, in an ambient of **-30 °C to +60 °C** and 5% to 95% relative humidity, non-condensing. Do not store an AX-600 where the coolant can freeze; the glycol mix protects to -30 °C but the pump seal is rated to -20 °C.

Recharge stored cabinets to 40% SoC every 6 months. A cabinet stored below 10% SoC for more than 90 days may suffer irreversible capacity loss, which is not covered by warranty.

## 5. Site acceptance

After setting the cabinet on the pad, complete the receiving record, verify the ground connection and proceed to AGS-DOC-005 for installation and AGS-DOC-006 for commissioning.
