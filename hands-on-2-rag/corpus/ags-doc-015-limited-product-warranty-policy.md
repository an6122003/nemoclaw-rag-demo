# Limited Product Warranty Policy

**Document ID:** AGS-DOC-015
**Revision:** E
**Effective date:** 2025-04-01
**Supersedes:** AGS-DOC-015 Rev D (2024-04-01)

## 1. Warranty periods

Warranty periods begin on the date of commissioning recorded in the Aurora Cloud site record, unless the site is not commissioned within 180 days of shipment, in which case the period begins on the shipment date. In no case does coverage extend beyond **12 years from the date of shipment**.

| Product | Part number | Period | Additional limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| AX-400 battery cabinet | AGS-AX400-372 | 10 years | 6,000 cycles at 80% DoD |
| AX-600 battery cabinet | AGS-AX600-558 | 10 years | 8,000 cycles at 80% DoD |
| Helios H3 PCS | AGS-H3-250 | 5 years | None |
| Comet C2 site controller | AGS-C2-SC | 5 years | None |
| Aurora Cloud edge gateway | AGS-EG-2 | 3 years | None |

Coverage ends at whichever limit is reached first. Cycle counts are taken from the Comet C2 on-box log, which is the authoritative record.

## 2. Energy retention guarantee

AGS guarantees that each AX-series cabinet will retain at least **70% of its nameplate usable energy at year 10** when operated within the published temperature envelope and within the maximum C-rate of 0.5C. Retention is measured by an AGS-witnessed capacity test performed at 25 °C. If a cabinet falls below the retention curve by more than 5 percentage points, AGS will repair or replace modules at its option.

## 3. What is covered

- Manufacturing defects in cells, modules, busbars, contactors, wiring and enclosure.
- Premature capacity loss beyond the retention curve.
- Coolant loop components on the AX-600, including the pump and filter housing.
- Firmware defects that cause a repeatable loss of a warranted function.

## 4. What is not covered

- Damage from operating above the rated temperature envelope, including operation of an AX-400 above +50 °C or an AX-600 above +50 °C.
- Damage from DC bus voltage outside the published operating range: 672-876 V for the AX-400 and 1,008-1,314 V for the AX-600.
- Damage from unauthorised firmware, including any firmware image not signed by AGS.
- Damage from connecting a battery module to the wrong cabinet model.
- Consumable items replaced on the maintenance schedule, except where a consumable failed within its published interval.
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function.

## 5. Obtaining service

Open a claim through the AGS support portal within **30 days** of discovering the defect. Warranty claims require the cabinet serial number, the site ID, the commissioning date and the fault log export. Approved claims are processed under AGS-DOC-016.

## 6. Transfer and extension

The warranty may be transferred to a new owner once, at no charge, by submitting a transfer notice with the site records. Extended coverage of an additional 5 years is available for AX-series cabinets purchased within the first 12 months of operation; contact AGS Sales for pricing.
