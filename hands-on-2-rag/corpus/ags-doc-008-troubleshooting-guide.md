# Troubleshooting Guide — AX Series, Helios H3 and Comet C2

**Document ID:** AGS-DOC-008
**Revision:** G
**Effective date:** 2025-08-01

## 1. Fault code index

| Code | Description | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| F-0101 | DC bus overvoltage | AX-400, AX-600 |
| F-0207 | Coolant flow low | AX-600 only |
| F-0312 | Fan failure | AX-400 only |
| F-0409 | Communications loss | All |
| F-0512 | Cell SoC imbalance | AX-400, AX-600 |
| F-0688 | Insulation fault | AX-400, AX-600, Helios H3 |

## 2. F-0207 — Coolant flow low (AX-600)

This fault exists only on the liquid-cooled AX-600. It is never generated by an AX-400, which is air cooled and has no coolant loop.

1. Confirm the coolant reservoir is at the nominal **38 L** fill and that the sight glass is bubble-free.
2. Check the coolant filter; replace with AGS-FILT-6603 if the differential pressure is high.
3. Verify pump operation. If the pump does not spin, replace AGS-PUMP-6602.
4. Reset the fault from the Comet C2. If it recurs within 24 hours, escalate to AGS Field Service.

## 3. F-0312 — Fan failure (AX-400)

This fault exists only on the forced-air AX-400. An AX-600 does not report F-0312.

1. Identify the failed fan from the cabinet diagnostic screen; the AX-400 has four axial fans.
2. De-energize the cabinet and wait the interval required by the governing safety bulletin (see AGS-DOC-010).
3. Replace the fan assembly with **AGS-FAN-2201**. Fan assemblies are not repairable in the field.
4. Restore power and confirm all four fans report normal speed. Operation with fewer than four running fans is limited to 50% of rated power and must not exceed 72 hours.

## 4. F-0512 — Cell SoC imbalance

A cell delta greater than 50 mV at rest triggers F-0512.

1. Verify the cabinet is at rest (no charge or discharge) for at least 30 minutes before reading cell voltages.
2. Run a full charge to 100% SoC and hold for 30 minutes to allow balancing.
3. If the imbalance persists above 50 mV after two balance cycles, isolate the affected rack and open an RMA for the suspect module: AGS-M48-100 on the AX-400, AGS-M48-120 on the AX-600.
4. Never swap a module between an AX-400 and an AX-600. The modules are not electrically or mechanically interchangeable.

## 5. F-0409 — Communications loss

1. Confirm the Comet C2 management address is reachable. The factory default is **192.168.10.40**.
2. Confirm the Modbus TCP port matches the controller firmware: **502** on 3.7.4 and 3.8.2 or later, **1502** on 3.8.0 and 3.8.1.
3. Check the CAN termination resistors at both ends of the trunk.
4. If a single device is missing, swap its CAN drop cable and re-poll.

## 6. Escalation

If a fault persists after the steps above, collect the full diagnostic bundle from Aurora Cloud and contact AGS Support. Critical site-down faults are acknowledged within 4 business hours.
