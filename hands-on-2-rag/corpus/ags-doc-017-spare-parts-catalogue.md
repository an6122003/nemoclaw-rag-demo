# Spare Parts Catalogue

**Document ID:** AGS-DOC-017
**Revision:** F
**Effective date:** 2025-08-20
**Currency:** All prices in USD, list, ex-works Boulder CO

## 1. How to order

Order spares through the AGS support portal or by email to spares@auroragrid.example. Quote the cabinet or PCS serial number with every order. Field-replaceable units (FRUs) marked *Certified* may only be installed by AGS-certified personnel; installing them otherwise voids the warranty on the affected assembly.

## 2. Battery modules

The two module part numbers are **not interchangeable**. A module ordered for an AX-400 will not fit, seat or communicate in an AX-600, and vice versa.

| Part number | Description | Used in | Energy | Weight | List price | Lead time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AGS-M48-100 | Battery module, 48 V, 100 Ah | AX-400 only | 4.8 kWh | 42 kg | $2,140 | 2 weeks |
| AGS-M48-120 | Battery module, 48 V, 120 Ah | AX-600 only | 5.76 kWh | 48 kg | $2,480 | 3 weeks |

Each AX-400 cabinet holds 8 modules across 4 racks; each AX-600 holds 12 modules across 6 racks.

## 7. Stocking recommendation

AGS recommends that every operating site hold one spare module per cabinet model in service, one AGS-FILT-6603 per AX-600, and one AGS-CTRL-3300 per site. Sites with more than 20 cabinets should hold one AGS-PUMP-6602 per 10 AX-600 cabinets.

All FRUs ship with a one-year parts warranty from the shipment date, independent of the cabinet warranty. Core items are subject to the return requirements in AGS-DOC-016.
